# StarkCircom brand

_Status: established in the deployment workbench_

StarkCircom uses a proof launch-console visual direction. Cool instrument surfaces, graphite structure, muted steel labels, and sparse Starknet coral keep attention on the next owner-approved operation. Green, amber, and red are reserved for operational state.

IBM Plex Sans is the display and body face. IBM Plex Mono is limited to hashes, class counts, evidence labels, and machine status.

`DESIGN.md` is the source of truth for tokens, layout, components, responsive behavior, and visual guardrails. `.impeccable/design.json` contains the matching machine-readable extensions and component previews.
